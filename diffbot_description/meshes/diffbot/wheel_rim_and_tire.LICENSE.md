# wheel_rim.stl and wheel_tire.stl: credit and terms

`wheel_rim.stl` and `wheel_tire.stl` in this folder are the wheel model [Wheel D65x25](https://grabcad.com/library/wheel-d65x25-1) by **KlimukVI**, published on GrabCAD on 2019-05-08 as "Wheel D65x25 for Arduino car project". They're the model's rim and tyre as two files, converted to STL in metres, with the axle along z. `../components/wheel.stl` is the same model as one file.

## Terms

The BSD 3-Clause licenses of the diffbot repository and of `diffbot_description` don't cover these files. GrabCAD's terms apply to them. GrabCAD's help article [How can models be used and shared?](https://help.grabcad.com/article/246-how-can-models-be-used-and-shared) sets them out:

- **Non-commercial public use**, where no money is exchanged, for example on social media, in a student presentation or in a free CAD library, is covered if you credit the model's original creator and link to the original model.
- **Commercial use** needs the original designer's explicit permission, and credit to both GrabCAD and the creator. Commercial use is exchanging the model or a design made from it for money, goods or services, selling prints of it, or using it in some way for a business or company. Without an answer from the creator, commercial use isn't allowed.

If you share these files or files made from them, keep this credit with them: the model's name, its creator KlimukVI and the link to the model.
